Research

ClinTrialBench

Co-author, under review at EACL 2027 (ACL Rolling Review)

A suite of three benchmarks for clinical trial and pharma quality tasks. I evaluated 22 models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and DeepSeek through LiteLLM, with and without reasoning modes. No single model led on all three.

SeverityBench Built by me
The model labels FDA-style inspection findings as Minor, Major or Critical and must cite the facts behind the label. Scored on accuracy, quadratic weighted kappa and evidence quality.
CapaBench Built by me
From a deviation narrative, the model lists the facts, finds the systemic root cause and writes corrective and preventive actions. Each part is graded by an LLM judge with precision, recall and F1.
SiteMonBench
The third benchmark in the suite, built by my co-authors.
